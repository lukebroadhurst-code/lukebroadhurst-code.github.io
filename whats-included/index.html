The property

What's in the Property

The nightly rate is for a place to live, not just a bed. Here is what we ask of a property, and what changes with the type of room, so a low rate doesn't come as a surprise.

We source managed, furnished, self-catering places. Ask us what is in each one before you choose.

Parking

Tell us what you drive, and we check parking before you decide.

This is what we ask for. A property can fall short, and when it does we want to hear about it the same day. Ask what is in the house before you choose, and we will tell you straight.

What Changes with the Room Type

Sharing, 2 to 4

A shared house with 2 to 4 of you, usually your own bedroom.

What you share. The kitchen, the living room and usually the bathroom. Ask how many bathrooms for how many of you.

What to watch. A rate far below what is typical for the area is a prompt to ask what is not in the house.

Own room

A room to yourself in a managed apartment, house or room-let.

What you share. In an apartment the kitchen and bathroom are yours. In a room-let you may share them. We say which before you choose.

What to watch. You are paying for space. If a single room is cheap for the area, ask what is missing.

Group of 5 or more

Larger houses for 5 or more, where the rate per person is usually lower.

What you share. A bigger kitchen and living space, and several bathrooms. Check bathrooms per head and where the vans go.

What to watch. Parking matters most here. A house for six with room for one van is a problem we would rather catch before you sign.

If a Rate Looks Low

  • Ask what is not in it. A rate well under the typical for the area can mean fewer extras.
  • Ask what the inventory covers. Kettle, pots, linen and towels are the basics to check.
  • Tell us the same day if something on the list is missing. We chase the property, with a photo if you can.

Questions

Questions About What's Included

Short answers. If yours isn't here, ask us.
Do we get a kettle, pots and linen?

That is the standard we ask for, and we tell you what each option has before you choose it.

Is parking included?

Cleaning and parking are in the rate in almost every booking, and if something is extra we tell you before you reserve. Tell us what you drive and we check the space suits it before you decide.

Who do our people ring when something goes wrong?

In office hours (Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00), they ring us on 01926 351855. Outside office hours, the same number goes to an out-of-hours line that a member of the Rooost team answers.

What if the property isn't as described?

Tell us the same day, with a photo if you can. We chase the property and, if it cannot be put right, we look for another place. How fast depends on who owns the fix, and we will tell you which.

Can our people have a say on where they stay?

Yes. Tell us what your crew care about: distance to site, own rooms, a proper kitchen, parking. We build the shortlist around it, so the place you pick is closer to the one they would pick.

Does anything cost extra?

Cleaning and parking are in the rate in almost every booking, and if something is extra we tell you before you reserve.
