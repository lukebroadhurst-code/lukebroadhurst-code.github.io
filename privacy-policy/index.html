In Plain English

  • We ask for your name, company, email address and phone number so we can reply to you. If you become a client or supplier, we also keep bank details and invoice records in Xero, our accounts system.
  • Your enquiry may be stored in HubSpot, our customer records system, and so may your name and email address if you join our mailing list. Only people who need your details can see them: management sees everything, other staff only what their job needs.
  • We sometimes contact people before they contact us. Those business details can come from databases such as Apollo and FullEnrich, or from public sources such as LinkedIn, and we use them only to contact you about workforce accommodation.
  • The policy gives our lawful reason for each use and how long we keep your details. Outside Rooost, details go only to our service providers, the property providers who host your team, and anyone the law requires us to tell. Some providers are US companies.
  • You can ask to see, correct, delete or limit our use of your details, object to it, or tell us to stop emailing or phoning you. Email hello@rooost.co.uk or call 01926 351855: we reply within one month. You can also complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk). For website cookies, see our Cookie Policy.

Valuing Privacy

We value your privacy and are committed to keeping your personal and business information safe. We use your personal information only for the purposes set out in this policy. We share it only with the service providers and property providers described in this policy, and where the law requires it.

How We Collect Information

We ask for contact information including your name, company name, email address and phone number via our website, so that we can reply to an enquiry you send us. If you accept analytics cookies, we use Google Analytics to see how people use our website. See our Cookie Policy. We collect your name and email address when you sign up for our electronic newsletter. We ask for your account and contact information when you hire services from us.

What Information We Hold

  • When you contact us by email or through our website, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and the company you work for
  • If you sign up for our electronic newsletter, we only collect your name and email address
  • If you are a client or customer, we also retain your business name and bank details for financial processing and we keep records of the invoices we send you and the payments you make within Xero (our preferred accountancy system)
  • If you are a supplier, we retain your business name and bank details for financial processing and we keep records of the invoices we receive from you and the payments we make within Xero (our preferred accountancy system)

Where We Store Your Information

If you contact us with a business enquiry, we may store your information in our Customer Relationship Management (CRM) software (HubSpot). If we do business together, we store your information in our accounts software (Xero). Opt in to our mailing list and your name and email address live in HubSpot too, which doubles as our e-marketing platform.

Because we work with contractors across the UK construction and infrastructure sector, we sometimes get in touch with people before they contact us. Where we do, your business contact details (typically your name, job title, company, work email address, phone number and LinkedIn profile) may have come from Apollo or FullEnrich, both business contact databases, or from publicly available sources such as LinkedIn or your company website. These details may be stored in Instantly, which we use for email outreach, and HeyReach, which we use for LinkedIn outreach.

We use these details for one purpose, which is to contact you about workforce accommodation. If you would like them corrected or removed, email hello@rooost.co.uk and we will sort it.

NB: We chose these systems partly for their commitment to security.

What We Use Your Information for, and Why

If you send us an enquiry or other form, we use your details to deal with it. We send you our newsletter, guides or other marketing emails only if you tick the box on the form, and you can unsubscribe at any time. Separately, we may email or phone businesses that have not contacted us about our products and services (see the lawful bases below), and if you tell us not to, we won’t get in touch again. We will use your information to send you invoices, statements, or reminders.

The law (UK GDPR) says we need a lawful reason, called a lawful basis, for each use of your information. These are ours:

  • Replying to an enquiry, a brief or a supplier application: our legitimate interests (a good business reason that does not override your rights) in answering you, and taking the steps you ask for before any contract.
  • Contracts and invoicing: carrying out our contract with you, and our legal duty to keep accounting and tax records.
  • Contacting businesses before they contact us: our legitimate interests in telling businesses that may need workforce accommodation what we do. You can object at any time (see Your Rights and How to Complain).
  • Our newsletter, and marketing emails you ask for by ticking a box on a form: your consent. You can withdraw it at any time with the unsubscribe link, or by emailing us.
  • Analytics and marketing cookies: your consent, given through the cookie banner. See our Cookie Policy.

How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the reason we collected it. Then we delete it or make it anonymous.

  • Enquiries, briefs and supplier applications: until the enquiry is dealt with, and afterwards for as long as we reasonably need to follow up or answer a question about it.
  • Clients and suppliers: contract, invoice and payment records for as long as the law requires us to keep company accounts and tax records, which is at least six years.
  • Business contacts we reach out to: until you tell us to stop, or until the details are out of date or no longer relevant to the work we do.
  • Newsletter and marketing emails: until you unsubscribe.

Who’s Responsible for Your Information at Our Company

Rooost Ltd (company number 14243445, 1 Mill St, Leamington Spa CV31 1ES) is responsible for your information. In law that makes us the data controller.

We have a nominated person responsible for the security of your information. You can contact that person by email at hello@rooost.co.uk or by phone on +44(0)1926 351855 if you have any concerns about the information we store.

Who Has Access to Information About You

When we store information, only the people who need it have access. Our management team has access to everything you’ve provided, but individual employees have access to only what they need to do their job.

Outside Rooost, your information goes only to:

  • the service providers named in this policy, who handle it on our behalf: HubSpot (customer records and email marketing), Xero (accounts), Instantly (email outreach) and HeyReach (LinkedIn outreach). If you accept analytics cookies, Google also receives usage data (see our Cookie Policy);
  • the property owners and operators who provide the accommodation we arrange, who need names and arrival details so your team can get in and be looked after;
  • anyone the law requires us to tell, such as HMRC, a court or a regulator.

Sending Information Outside the UK

Some of the providers named in this policy are US companies, or use servers outside the UK. So your information can be processed in other countries. The UK GDPR allows that only with a safeguard in place, such as the UK government approving the destination country, or contract terms that bind the provider to UK data protection standards. Ask us which safeguard applies to a provider and we will tell you, and send you a copy of it where you are entitled to one.

The Steps We Take to Keep Your Information Private

Where we store your information in third-party services, we restrict access only to people who need it. We store passwords in an encrypted password manager and use a different, randomly generated password for each service. Please note, we never use the same password twice.

The computers we use are protected by a passcode or fingerprint access. These computers ask for authentication whenever they’re started or after five minutes of inactivity. Our mobile devices are also protected by a fingerprint or facial recognition.

Your Rights and How to Complain

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong;
  • ask us to delete it;
  • ask us to limit how we use it;
  • object to us using it, including for marketing. If you object to marketing, we stop;
  • ask for a copy of what you gave us in a form you can pass to someone else, where that right applies;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on it.

To use any of these rights, email hello@rooost.co.uk or phone us. We will reply within one month.

We take complaints very seriously. If you’ve any reason to complain about the ways we handle your privacy, please contact via:
Email: hello@rooost.co.uk
Phone us on: +44(0)1926 351855
In writing: 1 Mill St, Leamington Spa CV31 1ES.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator: ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113.
