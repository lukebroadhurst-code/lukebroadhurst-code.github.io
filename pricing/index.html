Ex VAT and Inc VAT, Explained

Ex VAT is the rate before tax. Inc VAT is the same rate plus 20%, which is what your accounts team pays. We lead with ex VAT because that is how rates are normally compared.

Whether you can reclaim the VAT depends on your own position, so check with your accountant.

Serviced or rented

Serviced Is Not the Same as a Standard Rental

A rent figure from a letting site, a search or a chatbot is for a room. A serviced rate is for a place to live, with the running costs covered and someone to ring. Put them side by side and they won't match.

A Standard Rental

  • The room or the house, and nothing else
  • You add the bills, wifi, council tax, linen and cleaning
  • A tenancy with a fixed term, so you carry the cost if the job ends early
  • Nobody to ring when the boiler goes

A Serviced Nightly Rate

  • A serviced rate puts running costs into one price, so it will not match a rent figure.
  • Cleaning and parking are in the rate in almost every booking, and if something is extra we tell you before you reserve.
  • Furnished and managed, with someone to ring

Being straight

When a Hotel Is the Better Call

We would rather lose a booking than sell you the wrong thing. Here is the rule of thumb. It is guidance, not a guarantee, and it shifts with the town and the month.
  1. 01

    Short stays

    Under 4 nights, a hotel will usually be the better call.

  2. 02

    4 nights or more

    Stays of 4 nights or more suit us best. That is where a house starts to make sense: a bed each, a kitchen, and parking for the van.

  3. 03

    Where hotels do come in

    We do not sell hotel stays. We use a hotel only in an emergency, or when dates do not line up (for example, one night while a property is booked out).

A hotel rate is per room and ours is per person, so compare like with like. Not sure which side of the line you are on? Ask us. We will tell you straight, even if the answer is a hotel you book yourself.

Questions

Pricing Questions, Answered

Short answers. If yours isn't here, ask us.
Why is your rate higher than a rent I found?

A serviced rate puts running costs into one price, so it will not match a rent figure.

Rent is for the room. A serviced rate adds the running costs and someone to ring when something goes wrong. See serviced versus a standard rental.

Is VAT included?

No. Rates are per person, per night, before VAT. Add 20% to get the rate including VAT.

Whether you can reclaim the VAT depends on your own position, so check with your accountant.

Do you charge a booking fee?

One all-in nightly rate. No booking fee on top. We're paid by the properties we place you in. Our terms and conditions set out how payment and commission work.

Is the rate per person or per property?

Per person, per night, for a typical crew house. For something like a one-bed flat for two, a quote may be per property, and it says so. Either way, you see one number before you commit.

What if the crew changes size?

Tell us as soon as you know. The rate stays per person, and we will tell you if a different house would suit better.

When is a hotel the better call?

Under 4 nights, a hotel will usually be the better call. We do not sell hotel stays. We use a hotel only in an emergency, or when dates do not line up (for example, one night while a property is booked out). See the rule of thumb.

Can I forward this page to my finance team?
