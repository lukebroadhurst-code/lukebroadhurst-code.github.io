      Hotels Are Right Sometimes

      Under 4 nights, a hotel will usually be the better call. Book the hotel and don't give us a second thought. We don't sell hotel stays ourselves. We earn our place when the crew is away longer and the commute, the missing kitchen and the bill start to add up.

      Does It Cost More?

      We can't promise the cheapest night, and we won't pretend to. The nightly rate is one number on the invoice. Check the typical rate for your place, then count the VAT, the admin and what happens when the job moves.

      Questions? Ask us

      Quick Answers

      What does Rooost actually do?
      We find, reserve and look after houses and apartments for crews working away from home. You send one brief: where, when and how many of you. We send back a curated shortlist, you choose, and we handle the booking and the stay.
      Where can you house a crew?
      Across the UK. We know some places better than others, which is why the typical rates on this page cover just the places we have invoiced recently. On remote sites and islands supply is thinner, and we'll tell you that up front.
      Do we have to use the portal?
      No. Ring us on 01926 351855 or email hello@rooost.co.uk (Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00) and we'll deal with it. A client portal where you see shortlists, bookings, invoices and reporting is there if you want it.

      Pick one site, one crew, one set of dates. We'll show you what Rooost looks like on a real job, and if we can't help, we'll tell you straight.
