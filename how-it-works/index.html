How it works

Four Steps, and Most of Them Are Ours

You brief us. We find and check the places. Your crew move in, and your finance team gets one invoice. Here is what each step asks of you, what we do, and what can still go wrong.

  1. 01Brief
  2. 02Shortlist
  3. 03Reserved
  4. 04Reported
Start with one project See pricing and terms

01Brief

Where, When and How Many

One call, or one short form. No spec sheet, and no account needed to ask.

What you do

Ring us or send the brief. Where's the site? When does it start? How many of you? Add whatever decides it: vans, own rooms, a dog.

A short brief, by form or one call. Don't know the start date yet? Say so.

What we do

A real person reads it and asks the one or two things you would have forgotten. Then we start looking, across the properties and hosts we already work with and new ones where we need them.

What you get back

  • Confirmation that we have your brief
  • A named account manager who picks up the phone
  • Questions back, if the brief leaves a gap

What could slip, and what we do about it

A thin brief makes a thin shortlist. If we don't know about the vans, or who won't share a room, the options miss. We ask early, and you can change the brief at any point.

Under 4 nights, a hotel will usually be the better call. We would rather tell you than sell you the wrong thing.

Tell us what you need

02Shortlist

A Curated Shortlist

A few options, each one already matched to your brief.

What you do

Look at the options. Each shows distance to site, bedrooms and bathrooms per head, parking and the nightly rate. Pick one, or tell us what's off. See a shortlist in the portal.

Want a different mix, such as own rooms for some and a shared house for the rest? Say so and we'll rework it.

What we do

We match each option to your brief and say plainly what each one has, and what it hasn't. If a rate looks low for the area, we tell you what that usually means.

What you get back

  • Options back fast
  • Every option with distance to site, beds, baths and parking
  • One nightly rate per option, ex VAT. How pricing works

What could slip, and what we do about it

Speed, mostly. Rural sites and short notice take longer, and a good property can be let to someone else while you decide. If your dates are firm, tell us early.

Some places don't have much to offer. If we can't match your brief, we say so rather than pad the list.

See typical rates by place

03Reserved

Say Yes, and We Hold the Place

You choose. We deal with the property from here.

What you do

Tell us which option. Send a PO number if your finance team uses them.

If you are new to us, ask what we need before check-in.

What we do

We confirm with the property and keep the relationship with the host for the whole stay. Extensions, swaps and moves come to us, not to you.

We deal with the host before you arrive, so your crew don't find out at the door that nobody was expecting them.

What you get back

  • A booking confirmation by email, with the property details
  • Check-in details are sent ahead of your arrival
  • Who to ring if anything is off

What could slip, and what we do about it

Arrival details. Check-in times differ by property, and details from a host can arrive late. We send them ahead and chase if they haven't. If yours haven't reached you by the afternoon before, ring us.

A property can also fall through between your yes and your keys: a double booking, a boiler. If it does, we find another place and tell you straight away. How quickly depends on what's free near site.

What happens on arrival day

04Reported

One Invoice, Laid Out Your Way

The part your finance people feel.

What you do

Check the invoice. If your finance team needs a PO number or a project code on it, tell us up front.

What we do

A VAT invoice in your company's name for every stay. The host side stays off your plate.

What you get back

  • Invoices set up the way your accounts team needs them. Ask us.
  • A client portal where you see shortlists, bookings, invoices and reporting, with phone and email whenever you would rather

What could slip, and what we do about it

Invoices are where small mistakes show up: a PO number that changed, an extension, dates that overlap. Check the first one properly and tell us. If something is wrong, we correct it and reissue.

The portal is still being improved, so phone and email work just as well. Use whichever your team prefers.

Behind the scenes

The Work You Didn't See

Most of the effort in a booking doesn't reach your desk. That is the point of paying someone to do it.

Places We Drop

Too far from site. Nowhere for the vans. One bathroom for five people. A host who won't take a crew. You don't see them.

Questions We Chase

Is there room for a long-wheelbase van? Is the kettle there? Will the host say yes to a crew? We get the answers so you don't have to.

What Lands on Your Desk

A short list where every option already fits the brief, and a plain note on anything that doesn't. You sifted none of the rest.

    Check-in details are sent ahead of your arrival

Questions

Questions People Ask First

Short answers. If yours isn't here, ask us.
How long until we see options?

Options back fast. Rural sites, short notice and big crews take longer, and we will tell you early if that is you.

Do we need an account before we ask?

No. Ring us or send the brief and we will take it from there. If your procurement team needs paperwork first, see buying from Rooost.

What do you need from us to get started?

The site postcode, the start date, how many of you, how many nights a week, and what you drive. Anything that decides it, such as own rooms or a dog, helps too.

No start date yet? Tell us anyway.

What if our plans change after we've booked?

Tell us as soon as you know. Extensions, swaps and moves come to us, and we sort them with the property. Notice and cancellation terms are on the pricing page.

Who do we ring when something goes wrong?

In office hours (Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00), ring your account manager or the office on 01926 351855. Outside office hours, the same number goes to an out-of-hours line that a member of the Rooost team answers.

Report it with a photo or a short video and we chase the property. If it cannot be fixed, we look for another place. Some faults are sorted in minutes and others wait on a plumber or a landlord. We will tell you which.

Do you do hotels?

We do not sell hotel stays. We use a hotel only in an emergency, or when dates do not line up (for example, one night while a property is booked out).

Our work is houses and apartments for crews who stay a while. Stays of 4 nights or more suit us best. Under 4 nights, a hotel will usually be the better call. We would rather say so than sell you the wrong thing. See when a hotel is the better call.

Can we still use our travel desk or booking tool?

Yes. Keep using it for hotels and trains. We are for the crews who stay a while, and we work alongside your travel desk rather than replace it.

Does the portal replace talking to someone?

No. Ring or email whenever you like. The portal is there to save you chasing, not to hide us.

What if we need somewhere this week?

Ring us on 01926 351855 rather than filling in a form. Short notice is possible, but supply is thinner, and we will be straight with you about what is out there.
