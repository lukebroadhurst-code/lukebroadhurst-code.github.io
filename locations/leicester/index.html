The honest bits: what moves supply and prices around, and what to check before you reserve.

Worth Knowing Before You Reserve

  • Prices

    Prices vary a lot between the city and the villages around it, so ask for a rate by postcode.

  • Parking

    Crews with a pick-up truck need off-road parking, so say what you drive.

Who Works Here

Two kinds of crew commonly work in Leicester. Here's what each tends to need.

  • Construction and civil engineering

    Site teams on programmes that run for weeks or months. Van parking, a proper kitchen and enough bathrooms usually decide it.

  • Archaeology and heritage

    Teams on rural digs and surveys. Drying space, a real kitchen and flexible stay lengths go a long way.

Questions

Common Questions About Leicester

Straight answers for crews heading to Leicester. Something missing? Ask us.
How quickly can you find somewhere in Leicester?

Tell us where the site is, when it starts and how many of you there are. We come back with a short list matched to your brief, not a page of links.

What we aim for: options back fast.

Need it sooner? Ring us on 01926 351855, Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00. Or see how it works.

What if the project moves or the dates change?

Programmes shift. We'd rather you rang with a rough idea than waited for a perfect plan.

Tell us as early as you can and we will talk you through the options.

Terms in writing are on the pricing and terms page.

Can we have our own rooms in Leicester?

Yes. Say so in your brief and we shortlist for it. Most of our recent stays here have been shared houses, so we have fewer own-room examples to show and we'd quote it for your dates. Own rooms usually cost more per person than a share.

Bathrooms matter as much as bedrooms, so tell us how many of you can share one. Worth reading first: the "Parking" note above for Leicester.

Do you do short stays in Leicester?

Under 4 nights, a hotel will usually be the better call.

Stays of 4 nights or more suit us best. A managed house starts to make sense from there.

We do not sell hotel stays. We use a hotel only in an emergency, or when dates do not line up (for example, one night while a property is booked out).

Not sure where you sit? Give us your dates and we'll tell you straight.
