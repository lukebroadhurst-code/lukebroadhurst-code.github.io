Common Questions About Dingwall and Tain

Straight answers for crews heading to Dingwall and Tain. Something missing? Ask us.
How quickly can you find somewhere in Dingwall and Tain?

We find places in Dingwall and Tain job by job, so there is no shelf of ready properties to point at. Give us the site, the dates and your numbers, and we start looking from there. Worth reading first: the "Supply" note above for Dingwall and Tain.

What we aim for: options back fast. Some things take time. We'll tell you upfront which ones.

Need it sooner? Ring us on 01926 351855, Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00. Or see how it works.

What if the project moves or the dates change?

A start date that slips, a job that runs over, a crew that grows: all normal, and all easier to sort when we hear early.

Out here a good property can be let to someone else while you decide, so don't wait for certainty.

Tell us as early as you can and we will talk you through the options.

Terms in writing are on the pricing and terms page.

Can we have our own rooms in Dingwall and Tain?

Often, yes, though supply is thinner here, so say so early. We don't have a typical own-room rate for Dingwall and Tain yet, so we'd quote it for your dates.

Tell us how many of you can share a bathroom while you're at it.

Do you do short stays in Dingwall and Tain?

Under 4 nights, a hotel will usually be the better call.

Stays of 4 nights or more suit us best. A managed house starts to make sense from there.

We do not sell hotel stays. We use a hotel only in an emergency, or when dates do not line up (for example, one night while a property is booked out).

If you're not sure which side of the line you're on, tell us your dates and we'll say.
