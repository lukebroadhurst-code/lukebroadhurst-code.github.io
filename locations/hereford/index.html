Questions

Common Questions About Hereford

Straight answers for crews heading to Hereford. Something missing? Ask us.
How quickly can you find somewhere in Hereford?

We find places in Hereford job by job, so there is no shelf of ready properties to point at. Give us the site, the dates and your numbers, and we start looking from there. Worth reading first: the "Supply" note above for Hereford.

What we aim for: options back fast. Some things take time. We'll tell you upfront which ones.

Need it sooner? Ring us on 01926 351855, Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00. Or see how it works.

What if the project moves or the dates change?

Programmes shift. We'd rather you rang with a rough idea than waited for a perfect plan.

A good property can be let to someone else while you decide, so don't wait for certainty.

Tell us as early as you can and we will talk you through the options.

Terms in writing are on the pricing and terms page.

Can we have our own rooms in Hereford?

Often, yes. Say so early, because we find places job by job here. We don't have a typical own-room rate for Hereford yet, so we'd quote it for your dates.

Bathrooms matter as much as bedrooms, so tell us how many of you can share one.

Do you do short stays in Hereford?

Under 4 nights, a hotel will usually be the better call.

Stays of 4 nights or more suit us best. A managed house starts to make sense from there.

We do not sell hotel stays. We use a hotel only in an emergency, or when dates do not line up (for example, one night while a property is booked out).

If you're not sure which side of the line you're on, tell us your dates and we'll say.
