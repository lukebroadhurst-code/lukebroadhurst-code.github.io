Worth Knowing Before You Reserve

  • Seasons

    Islands are popular holiday destinations, so booking sites often show nothing free in summer.

  • Ferries and flights

    Crossings are weather dependent, so allow for delays before you plan the first night.

Plan the beds around the boat or the flight, not the other way round. Give us your dates early and we'll work back from the crossing.

Questions

Common Questions About Shetland

Straight answers for crews heading to Shetland. Something missing? Ask us.
How quickly can you find somewhere in Shetland?

Islands are slower than the mainland. Supply is thin and travel depends on the weather, so the earlier you brief us, the better the options. Worth reading first: the "Seasons" note above for Shetland.

What we aim for: options back fast. Some things take time. We'll tell you upfront which ones.

Need it sooner? Ring us on 01926 351855, Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00. Or see how it works.

What if the project moves or the dates change?

Dates move on most jobs, and the earlier we hear, the more we can do about it.

Ferries and flights move with the weather, so tell us about travel changes too. Worth reading first: the "Ferries and flights" note above for Shetland.

Tell us as early as you can and we will talk you through the options.

Terms in writing are on the pricing and terms page.

Can we have our own rooms in Shetland?

Often, yes, though supply is thinner here, so say so early. We don't have a typical own-room rate for Shetland yet, so we'd quote it for your dates.

Say how many of you are happy to share a bathroom too. It narrows the shortlist fast.

Do you do short stays in Shetland?

Under 4 nights, a hotel will usually be the better call.

Stays of 4 nights or more suit us best. A managed house starts to make sense from there.

We do not sell hotel stays. We use a hotel only in an emergency, or when dates do not line up (for example, one night while a property is booked out).

If you're not sure which side of the line you're on, tell us your dates and we'll say.
