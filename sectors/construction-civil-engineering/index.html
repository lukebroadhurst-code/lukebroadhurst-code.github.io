Housing a crew

What Construction Crews Actually Need

  • A Room Each, a Bathroom to Spare

    Operatives will share a house. Many won't share a room. Tell us the split and we'll look for houses with the bedrooms and bathrooms to match the head count, not just the beds.

  • Somewhere for the Vans

    Vans, trailers and tools need parking that fits and feels safe. Give us the height and length, and whether kit stays in overnight. Parking goes on the brief, not in the small print.

  • Kit to Dry, Food to Cook

    Wet gear needs somewhere to dry. A crew cooking for six needs a proper oven and more than one pan. Tell us it matters, and kitchen, washing and drying become part of the brief.

  • Monday to Friday, or All Seven

    Five nights can look cheaper until the crew moves house every weekend. In some places a continuous stay works out cheaper per night, so ask us to price both.

  • Programmes That Move

    Starts slip. Crews grow and shrink. Tell us the dates are soft and we'll plan the brief around it, and say plainly what a change would cost before you commit.

  • A Drive You Can Live With

    A cheap house is rarely a close one. We start from the site postcode and say how far each option really is, so a long drive isn't a surprise on Monday morning.

Straight answers

What Goes Wrong, and What We Do

Four things we hear again and again, how we handle each, and where we stop.
  1. 01

    The Booking Lands on Whoever's Nearest

    What we do

    You send one brief. We find the options, chase the owners and sort the paperwork. You decide who goes where, and you stop being the accommodation helpline.

    What we can't promise

    We take the searching and the chasing. We can't take decisions that are yours, like who shares a house with whom.

  2. 02

    Card Statements Nobody Can Add Up

    What we do

    A VAT invoice in your company's name for every stay. Tell us what your accounts team needs on an invoice, and we will say plainly what we can do.

    What we can't promise

    How invoices are split depends on how your accounts team works, so say so on the first call. Ask us about payment terms for a first booking.

  3. 03

    Hosts Who Say No to Subcontractors

    What we do

    Some hosts turn crews and subcontractors away. Ask us how we handle it. Check-in details are what crews chase, so we chase them for you.

    What we can't promise

    Arrival day is where things can slip. If the details haven't arrived, ring us. Don't wait for the host.

  4. 04

    The Programme Moves and the Beds Don't

    What we do

    Tell us the dates are soft and the numbers might change. We plan the stay around that, and tell you what a change would cost before you say yes.

    What we can't promise

    We can't make an owner hold a house for ever. Good properties get re-let while a decision is pending, so the day your start firms up, ring us.

Honest fit check

Is Rooost Right for This?

We'd rather tell you now than after a long sourcing job.

A good fit

  • Stays of 4 nights or more suit us best.
  • Jobs that run for weeks, or come round again.
  • A brief that changes: more people, new dates, another town.

A hotel is simpler

  • Under 4 nights, a hotel will usually be the better call.
  • A single night in a town you won't be back to. Book the hotel.

Booking direct

Booking direct with an owner can cost less per night. If you've got a landlord you trust and someone to manage them, keep them. We can't promise the cheapest nightly rate. We can take the admin, the chasing and the late calls off your desk.

Questions

Questions Crews Ask Us

Who picks the property, the office or the site manager?
You do, from a shortlist. We send a few options with the drive time and what each property has. Tell us who has the final say, whether that's the site manager or a director, and we'll make sure they see it too.
Will my crew get a room each?
Many crews want their own bedroom and share the kitchen and bathrooms. Say so on the brief, with the bathrooms you need, and we'll look for it. A room to yourself usually costs more per person than sharing, and the rates on this page show both.
Does it cost less than booking direct?
Not always, and we won't pretend it does. Booking direct can cost less per night. From us you get the searching, the chasing and one point of contact. A VAT invoice in your company's name for every stay, not a pile of receipts. Our typical rates by place let you compare like with like.
What paperwork do we get, and who deals with it?
Tell us what your accounts team needs on an invoice, and we will say plainly what we can do. Supplier forms, insurance and payment terms are on the procurement page. Running several houses on one programme? Ask us about one PO for the lot.
What if the start date moves?
Tell us as soon as you know. Dates move on many programmes, and good properties get re-let while a decision is pending, so an early call gives us more to work with. We'll tell you what a change would cost before you agree. Ask us what applies.
