Housing a crew

What Ground Engineering Crews Actually Need

  • Start From the Site Postcode

    Rigs and trenches go where the ground is. We start from the site postcode, not the nearest big town, and say how far each option really is. If anyone can't drive, tell us, because some places need a walk to site.

  • Week by Week, Then Another Week

    Many jobs book a week or a fortnight at a time and move on with the rig. Tell us what's firm and what's a guess, and we'll build the brief around both.

  • A Room Each in Mixed Crews

    A supervisor, two drillers and an engineer may not want to share. We'll say up front if a house needs sharing to work, so there's no surprise on arrival.

  • Vans, Trailers and Kit

    Drilling vans, trailers and tools need parking in sight of the front door. Tell us what you tow and whether kit stays in overnight. Owners' descriptions of parking can be wrong for a big van, so give us the length and height.

  • Mud, Long Days, Proper Food

    Boots come in muddy and shifts end late. An oven or air fryer, somewhere to dry kit and a washing machine are worth asking for. Say which, and they go on the brief.

  • Seasons Change the Choice

    Coastal and Highland places fill with visitors in summer, and the choice thins out. If your job runs through it, brief us early and expect fewer options.

Straight answers

What Goes Wrong, and What We Do

Four things we hear again and again, how we handle each, and where we stop.
  1. 01

    The Nearest Beds Are Full, or Too Far

    What we do

    Give us the site postcode and the dates. We'll tell you what's close, what's a longer drive, and where there's nothing, so you can plan around it.

    What we can't promise

    Remote sites have thin supply. We can't conjure a house in a village that hasn't got one, but we'll say so early, not on Sunday night.

  2. 02

    A Hotel Is the Fallback, and It Adds Up

    What we do

    For a stay of 4 nights or more, a house with a room each is often a better fit than separate hotel rooms, and it comes with a kitchen. We'll show you the house options so you can compare.

    What we can't promise

    Under 4 nights, a hotel will usually be the better call. We'd rather say that than sell you a house that doesn't make sense. One-night house stays are hard to place.

  3. 03

    Dates That Follow the Weather

    What we do

    Tell us at the start that the finish is a forecast. It changes which houses we show you, and it's much easier to plan for than to fix on the day.

    What we can't promise

    Some owners can run on and some can't. If yours can't, we'll look for somewhere close that can, and tell you as early as we can.

  4. 04

    One Property, One Opinion

    What we do

    We send a shortlist, not a single answer, with the drive time and what each property has. Forward it to whoever has the final say, including the crew.

    What we can't promise

    More choice takes a little more time to decide, and good properties get re-let. If you need a yes by Friday, tell us on Monday.

Questions

Questions Crews Ask Us

Do you cover rural and coastal sites?
We try. Rural supply is thin, so there we may take longer or have fewer choices. Tell us the site postcode and we'll say what's near, what's a longer drive and where we'd need to ask around. Remote places can be a job-by-job quote, and we'll tell you which up front.
Can you do a week at a time?
Many briefs come that way, and extensions are common on ground work. Tell us the first week is firm and the rest is a guess. Some owners can run on and some can't, and we'll say which when we show you options. Ask us what applies to your project.
How quickly can you find somewhere?
Options back fast, but we won't promise a time we can't keep. A remote site with thin supply takes longer than a town. Starting soon? Ring us on 01926 351855 and say so.
Do crews get their own room?
Where the brief asks for it, we look for it. Say a room each, and we'll look for houses with the bedrooms and bathrooms to match. A room to yourself usually costs more per person than sharing a house, and the rates on this page show both.
We already use hotels and a travel desk. Do we have to change that?
No. Keep it for stays under 4 nights. Houses start to make sense from 4 nights up. We can sit alongside your travel desk or booking platform, so nothing else has to change. Start with one project.
