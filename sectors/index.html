Questions

Questions About Sectors

We're a building services or M&E contractor. Is this for us?
Often, yes. The needs are the same: own rooms, parking for vans, a short drive. Budgets on this kind of work can be tight, so tell us yours early and we'll say if it works. Specialist fit-out is covered on the data centres and facilities page.
Our sector isn't on the list. Who do you house?
Crews working away from home for several nights a week, for a few weeks or longer: utilities, remediation, fit-out, surveys, specialist trades and more. Tell us the site, the dates and how many of you. If we're not right, we'll say so.
We're a consultancy or professional services firm. Do you help?
If your people are working away on site, such as surveyors, engineers or inspectors, yes: the same brief applies. For office-based travel, a hotel or serviced apartment is usually simpler. Tell us what you need and we will say which.
Why are some sector pages shorter than others?
We write what we can stand behind. Data centres are newer ground for us, so that page says so. Everywhere else we show rates from what we invoiced, and we say Ask us where we don't have enough recent invoice lines.
How do I know the rates are real?
They come from invoices, not quotes: typical per person per night, excluding VAT, from stays in the last year. Each rate says how many stays it's based on. They're a guide, not a quote.

Not Sure Which Fits You?

Tell us the site, the dates and how many of you. We'll say which sector page matches your crew, or that none does and we'll work it out together.
