Your week

Sound Familiar?

You're running an electrical fit-out on a new data hall on the edge of a business park.

Eight specialists, five nights a week, for fourteen weeks. They're experienced, well paid and used to decent standards. A budget lodge with Wi-Fi that works in the car park isn't what they expect.

The client wants an approved supplier list. Your director wants a budget they can sign. And you'd like the same eight people there for the whole job.

An illustration, not a client story.

With Rooost

We don't have a data centre case study to show you, and we'd rather say so. What we can do is take a clear brief, tell you honestly what we can find near the site, and show you typical rates for the places we do work in.

Housing a crew

What Specialist Crews Expect

  • A Room That Shuts Out the Day

    Specialists expect their own room with a door that closes, and quiet if they've worked nights. We'll say honestly if a house needs sharing to work.

  • Somewhere to Work in the Evening

    Calls, drawings and reports don't stop at six. Tell us what good enough Wi-Fi and a workable table mean for your team, and we'll tell you what we can find. We won't promise a speed we haven't seen.

  • Secure Parking for Vans and Kit

    Test equipment and tools live in vans. Tell us what you carry and what you drive, and parking goes on the brief.

  • An Approved Supplier List to Join

    Big sites and their main contractors often keep approved supplier lists. If you need us on one, start early. What we supply is on the procurement page.

  • Phases Change the Headcount

    Fit-out, commissioning and handover each need a different number of people. Tell us the phases and we will plan around the changes.

  • Facilities and Maintenance Teams

    Planned shutdowns and call-outs mean short, scattered stays. Under 4 nights, a hotel is simpler. For a team away for a few weeks, a house starts to make sense.

Straight answers

What Goes Wrong, and What We Do

Four things we hear again and again, how we handle each, and where we stop.
  1. 01

    Specialists Expect Better Than a Basic Lodge

    What we do

    Tell us the standard you expect. We'll shortlist to it, and say plainly what each property has: bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchen, parking and Wi-Fi.

    What we can't promise

    We can't tell you what we haven't seen. Ask what's in a property before you reserve. Professional-grade, not luxury.

  2. 02

    A Supplier List, a Form and a Deadline

    What we do

    Start the paperwork alongside a first project. The procurement page says what we supply, so your team can answer without a call.

    What we can't promise

    Approval takes as long as your client's process takes. We can't speed it up, but we can make our side easy to read.

  3. 03

    A Budget That Needs a Director's Signature

    What we do

    Use the typical rates for places we work in to put a realistic number in front of whoever signs. We'll tell you early if your number won't work for the area.

    What we can't promise

    Budgets on this kind of work can be tight, and we'd rather tell you now than after a long sourcing job. If your budget is below what the area costs, we'll say so.

  4. 04

    Short Call-Outs and Scattered Stays

    What we do

    Under 4 nights, use a hotel. Where a facilities team is away for weeks, or a project runs in phases, a house can work, and we'll plan it with you.

    What we can't promise

    A string of one-night stays isn't something we do well, and we'll say so.

Honest fit check

Is Rooost Right for This?

A good fit

  • Stays of 4 nights or more, away for weeks, on fit-out or commissioning.

  • Specialist crews who expect their own room and a decent place to work.

A hotel is simpler

Questions

Questions Crews Ask Us

Have you housed crews on data centre projects?
Not enough to put a case study on this page, and we won't invent one. Our track record so far is in other sectors. Tell us the site and we'll be straight about what we can do.
Can you meet the standard our specialists expect?
Tell us the standard in plain words: own room, bathrooms per head, kitchen, parking, Wi-Fi, a place to work. We'll say what we can find and what we can't. We won't promise a finish or a speed we haven't seen. Professional-grade, not luxury.
Our client needs an approved supplier. How do we start?
Start with the procurement page and send it to your procurement team. Approval can take longer than the project allows, so it can run alongside a first small project. We can't speed up your client's process, and we won't pretend to.
What would it cost?
The typical rates on this site are for places crews have booked, not for data centre sites. Look at typical rates by place for the area nearest yours. Where we don't have enough recent stays to show a rate, we say Ask us and quote job by job.
Do you do short or scattered stays for facilities teams?
Not well. Under 4 nights is usually simpler in a hotel. Where a team is away for a few weeks, or a project has phases, a house can work, so tell us the pattern and we'll say honestly.
